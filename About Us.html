About Us

Food of The Gods, Freshly Baked

Since 2024, we've been serving our guests the best quality treats, traditionally made and presented with care.

AUTHENTIC RECIPES

Our products are based on traditional home-style recipes using fresh ingredients.

BAKED WITH LOVE

Our passion for baking is poured into every recipe, serving smiles on a plate everyday.

COMMITTED TO QUALITY

From our ingredients to our kitchen operations and guest services, we always prioritize quality.

HONESTLY PRICED

We constantly strive to offer the best products at the right prices.

The dessert house would be a dedicated establishment for sweet foods and indulgent range of offerings that include brownies, cakes, desserts, chocolates, breads, and savouries.

From our humble beginnings in 2024, when the first dessert house pastry store opened its place of Karakudi, Tamil Nadu, we've grown to become a Pan-India chain of patisseries with stores in over 10 cities.

Our mission is to spread happiness by serving smiles on a plate, and this journey continues as we open dessert house patisseries across the country.

We as a brand believe in the power of wishes.

Remember the days when you believed anything was possible?

When dreams felt like they were within reach? We want to bring that sense of wonder back to your lives.

With every cake we create, we aim to spark joy and remind you that dreams do come true.

At the heart of our journey is constant innovation. Our in-house R & D centre helps us discover new flavours, reimagine classic desserts, and craft experiences that delight with every bite.

This commitment to creativity and quality has not only won hearts but also earned us industry recognition, including the Swiggy Award for Best Dessert Brand — a milestone that sweetly affirms our mission.
